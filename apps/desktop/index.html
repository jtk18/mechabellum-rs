Preparing game art

Reading unit, tech, and specialist art from your Mechabellum install. This only happens once.

0%

    Set up in Testing Grounds

    • It drives the game with your mouse and keyboard for a few minutes. Move the mouse to stop it.
    • It starts the game if it isn't running, and only uses Solo, Testing Grounds.
    • In the game's Settings, Control, turn on Increase max camera height, with its slider at the right end.
    • The game's picture must be 16:10: fullscreen at 1920×1200 or 2560×1600, or a 16:10 window.

    macOS has to allow Mechabellum Stats to do this, under System Settings, Privacy & Security:

    Ask macOS, then switch Mechabellum Stats on in each list. Screen Recording takes effect after the app restarts.
