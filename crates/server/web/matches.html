How deployments are classed
  • Aggressive: units whose front edge is within 5 squares of the line, or in a flank column.
  • Midboard: units from the aggressive band back to level with the towers' centers.
  • Backboard: units behind the towers' centers.
  • "The line" is the front edge of a side's deployment zone, a square short of the middle.
  • Each unit counts by the ground it covers. An army is aggressive or backboard when that band holds most of its ground and at least 40%; otherwise it's midboard.
  • A midboard army is standard when its ground is spread across both sides, and asym mid aggro when three quarters or more of it stands on one side. An aggressive army is sym or asym by its aggressive ground the same way. Backboard isn't split.
  • Two asym armies on the same lane are an asym headbutt: they meet head on. On opposite lanes, each faces the other's empty side.
  • A match is classed by how each player deployed for round 1. Later fights are classed the same way on the fight tape, to show how the board grew. 1v1 games on the 2.0 board (build 2259 on).
