One card per shared match: both armies on the board, every fight's result and reactor HP, and how each player built and deployed. Pick a fight on the tape to see that round's board.

How deployments are classed
  • Aggressive: units whose front edge is within 3 squares of the line, or in a flank column.
  • Midboard: units right in front of the towers, from 5 squares ahead of their front faces back to level with their centers.
  • Backboard: units behind the towers' centers.
  • Each unit counts by the ground it covers. An army takes the class of the band holding most of it, when that's aggressive, midboard or backboard with at least 40% of it. Otherwise it's standard.
  • An aggressive or midboard army is sym when the units in that band span both sides of the board, asym when three quarters or more of them stand on one side. Standard and backboard aren't split.
  • A match is classed by how each player deployed for round 1. Later fights are classed the same way on the fight tape, to show how the board grew. 1v1 games on the 2.0 board (build 2259 on).
